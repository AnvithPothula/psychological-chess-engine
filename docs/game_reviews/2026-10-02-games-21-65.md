# Game review: games 21-65 (2 October 2026)

Every move of the 45 games since the first review, scored by Stockfish at depth
16 (`python -m src.eval.game_review`). All 45 were against bots, mostly through
the outbound challenger. Per-game details are in the appendix.

## Record

37 wins (33 by mate, 1 by resignation, 3 on the opponent's time), 6 draws,
2 losses, both on time. Blitz rating went from provisional to **2101** over 45
games, so opponents climbed from about 1500 to about 2100 within the day.

Against the stronger half, two separate problems showed up. Both are fixed.

## Problem 1: two more losses on time (fixed in `22d9ce4`)

[tq8SjaTx](https://lichess.org/tq8SjaTx) (bot at +1.33) and
[wczLemzW](https://lichess.org/wczLemzW) (+3.03), again as Black at 5+3, again
with the bot's clock never below its starting 300s. A third cause, separate from
the two fixed in `e3809bc`: after the bot moved, **the game stream went silent
for ten minutes without an error**. The connection was dead but nothing noticed.
The opponent's reply never arrived, the bot waited, and the clock ran out. Only
then did the read fail and the new reconnect logic start, too late.

berserk sets no timeout on any request. Every request now gets a 10s connect and
20s read timeout. Lichess streams send a keep-alive line every **7.0s**
(measured), so a healthy stream never trips it and a dead one is rejoined within
20s.

## Problem 2: won games thrown away (fixed in `f8a0c4a`)

Five of the six draws came from positions the bot was winning by +3.7 to +8.9,
after 67 to 208 plies (the sixth, 9ptYfue8, was level throughout):

| game | peak | the move | eval | logged utility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [lFXGOMOE](https://lichess.org/lFXGOMOE) | +3.71 | 29.Rxa4 | +3.71 → 0.00 | |
| [Nkc1568f](https://lichess.org/Nkc1568f) | +6.52 | 21.Ng5 | +6.09 → +1.40 | |
| [pWYsJoCP](https://lichess.org/pWYsJoCP) | +8.92 | 35...Bc6 | +6.38 → +0.20 | |
| [br6HThNm](https://lichess.org/br6HThNm) | +8.74 | 76...Kh6 | +7.48 → +0.49 | **+7584cp, TRAP** |
| [SuONy7gd](https://lichess.org/SuONy7gd) | +6.71 | 34...Bf4+ | +6.71 → +0.48 | **+3758cp, TRAP** |

Each draw is one move throwing the win away. The
logged ones were flagged TRAP with huge expected utility: the search bet on a
predicted blunder into mate, and 2000-rated engines do not make the errors
Maia predicts humans make.

Across all 45 games, **18 of the bot's 19 blunders came in positions it was
already winning**, and 12 of them dropped the advantage below +3. The 15 missed
forced mates are the same habit, cheaper: the bot was still +6 to mate afterwards.

The cause is the safety floor. It is absolute: a move may not drop below -1.80.
At +7 that allows giving back almost nine pawns for a predicted blunder.

**Fix:** once the best candidate is objectively +3 or more, only moves that stay
above +3 and within 2 pawns of the best are kept. Below +3 nothing changes, so
the trap search still runs where games are undecided. Replayed with the live
search on the 34 positions:

| | rule off | rule on |
|---|---|---|
| mean loss on the 18 blunder positions | 326cp | 14cp |
| still a blunder (300cp+) | 12 of 18 | 0 of 18 |
| forced mate kept | 6 of 15 | 12 of 15 |

With the rule off the replay reproduces the live moves (Kh6, Bf4+, Ng5, Bc6), so
these were systematic, not noise.
